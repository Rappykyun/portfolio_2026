# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are prospective freelance clients who need a developer for mixed software projects. They need to understand Ralph's range, judge delivery credibility, review relevant work, and submit a project inquiry quickly.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Ralph Vincent Rodriguez as a freelance software builder and converts qualified visitors into project inquiries. Success means that a visitor can understand the services, verify delivery evidence through case studies and live work, and send a useful project brief.

## Positioning

Ralph handles mixed software projects across web applications, mobile applications, data and machine-learning systems, and IoT systems. The portfolio differentiates him through deployed work, technical case studies, public-sector experience, and a verified data-engineering credential rather than unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive from referrals, social profiles, job and freelance platforms, or shared project links. They may scan the site on a phone before reviewing a case study on a larger screen. The main workflow is: understand the offer, inspect project proof, then submit an inquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing routes: home, projects, project case studies, and contact.
- Existing stack: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Vercel-compatible deployment.
- Core service areas: web, mobile, data and machine learning, and IoT.
- The main conversion action is a project inquiry delivered through Formspree.
- The site supports light and dark themes.
- The site must remain responsive and usable without motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ralph Vincent Rodriguez.
- Primary visual signal: green, refined into a modern high-contrast accent system.
- Tone: capable, direct, technical, and approachable.
- Inspiration: victoreke.com informs restraint, editorial spacing, and proof-led structure. Its exact assets, type treatment, colors, copy, logo, and animated mesh are not copied.
- The redesign uses an interactive creative-lab direction while keeping client outcomes clearer than visual effects.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five detailed software case studies with technologies, features, architecture information, live links, and source links where available.
- Deployed projects under `ralphvincent.tech` subdomains.
- DataCamp Certified Data Engineer Associate credential and certificate.
- CHED Regional Office XII experience.
- Existing portrait, CV, work logo, and custom technology icons in `public/` and application component folders.
- No testimonials, client metrics, pricing claims, or published service guarantees are available; the site must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with client problems and working outcomes, not a technology inventory.
2. Make every claim verifiable through a project, credential, live demo, or source link.
3. Show technical range without making the offer difficult to understand.
4. Use interaction to demonstrate craft, never to obstruct reading or inquiry.
5. Keep the path from first impression to project inquiry short.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio must meet WCAG 2.2 AA basics: semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, accessible form feedback, 44px touch targets, and a complete reduced-motion experience.
